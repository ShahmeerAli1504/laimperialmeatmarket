Preguntas Frecuentes

Questions We Hear Every Day

What are your hours?

Both stores are open 7 days a week. Winnemucca Blvd is open 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM, and South Bridge St opens early at 6:30 AM and closes at 8:00 PM.

Do both stores carry the same things?

Yes — both locations have a full meat market, taquería, panadería and Mexican grocery. Wild game processing is done at the South Bridge St store.

Can I order online or get delivery?

Yes. Our taquería menu and party packs are on DoorDash for delivery or pickup. DoorDash prices may differ from in-store prices.

Can you cut meat a special way or prepare a large order?

Absolutely. Ask our butchers at the counter for custom cuts, thickness and bulk orders for parties. For big orders, call ahead so we can have everything ready.

What kinds of meat do you carry?

Certified Angus Beef, pork, chicken, seafood and chorizo, plus house-marinated meats for carne asada, carnitas, barbacoa, lengua, tripas and menudo.

Do you do catering for graduations and events?

Yes. We help with graduation parties, family gatherings and special events — fresh cuts, carne asada options and Mexican food. Smaller party packs are on DoorDash; for bigger events, call either store and order early.

What do I need to know before bringing in wild game?

Animals must be cleaned and completely skinned, with no head, feet or organs attached. If you want to keep the head or hide, seal it in a plastic bag and box it. Call (775) 623-2405 with questions.

Is the bakery bread fresh?

Every day. Conchas, cuernos, cookies, cakes, donuts and seasonal pan dulce are baked fresh — come early for the best selection.

Where can I get breakfast early?

Our South Bridge St store opens at 6:30 AM — perfect for breakfast burritos and chilaquiles on your way to work.

Do you speak Spanish?

¡Claro que sí! Our team speaks English and Spanish, so feel free to call or visit in whichever you prefer.

Still have a question? We're happy to help.
